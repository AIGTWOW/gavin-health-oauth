Personal Health Integration

This site hosts the homepage and privacy policy for a personal, single-user application that connects to the Google Health API.

Purpose. The application lets its owner (a single individual) read their own Fitbit / Google Health data — sleep, resting heart rate, activity and body metrics — and combine it with data from other personal wearable devices for private health tracking.

Key facts

  • Single user. Only the developer's own Google account is authorised. No other users can sign in, and the application is not offered to the public.
  • Non-commercial. No advertising, no subscriptions, no resale of any kind.
  • Private storage. Retrieved data is stored only on the developer's own private server and is never transmitted to any third party.
  • Revocable at any time. The account owner can revoke access instantly from the Google Account permissions page.
